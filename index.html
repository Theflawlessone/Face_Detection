Data Loading

This section outlines how the UTKFace dataset was imported into the notebook. We loaded images along with key labels such as age, gender, and ethnicity. Careful handling ensured corrupted or missing files were detected early so the dataset could be cleanly prepared for further preprocessing and modeling stages.

Preprocessing

During preprocessing, images were resized, normalized, and converted into numerical arrays suitable for neural networks. Label values were encoded, invalid entries were removed, and the dataset was split into training, validation, and testing partitions to ensure fair evaluation. These steps standardized the data and improved model performance.

Modeling

In this section we built the neural network architectures used for age and gender prediction. Models incorporated convolutional layers for extracting features, pooling layers for reducing dimensionality, and fully connected layers for final predictions. Multiple architectures were constructed to compare performance and robustness.

Training

The training process involved feeding batches of images into the networks while optimizing the weights using backpropagation. We tracked metrics such as training and validation loss to ensure convergence and identify overfitting. Hyperparameters—including learning rate and batch size—were adjusted to maximize stability.

Evaluation & Results

This final section presents performance metrics including accuracy, mean absolute error, and confusion matrices. Visualizations from the notebook helped reveal strengths and weaknesses of each model, highlighting prediction patterns and identifying where generalization improved or declined.
